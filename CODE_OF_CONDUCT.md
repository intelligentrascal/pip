# Code of conduct

Pip welcomes contributors regardless of background or experience. Discuss ideas
respectfully, give specific constructive feedback and respect others' privacy.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing and
publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. Good-faith
technical disagreement is welcome; personal attacks are not.

The maintainer, **intelligentrascal**, is responsible for moderation. Maintainers may
remove harmful content, explain a requested correction, limit participation or ban
repeat or severe misconduct. They should apply these measures consistently and
protect reporters' confidentiality. Do not repost private reports in public.

Use a confirmed private maintainer channel to report conduct concerns. No private
conduct-reporting channel exists in this local preparation package; do not post
sensitive details publicly while that channel is being established. The security
vulnerability form is for vulnerabilities, not unrelated conduct reports.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone represents the project.
It does not claim to replace legal obligations or emergency services.
