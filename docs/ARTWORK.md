## Terms checked during preparation

- [Comfy Terms of Service](https://comfy.org/terms-of-service), effective May 13,
  2026: section 2 retains customer rights in inputs/outputs as between the parties;
  similar outputs may exist. Partner Nodes also apply the model provider's terms.
  The output disclaimer does not guarantee title or non-infringement.
- [BFL developer terms](https://bfl.ai/legal/developer-terms-of-service), sections
  3–5: describe customer output rights and personal/commercial use, but also
  restrict competing-model training and removal/alteration of Content Credentials.
  Their application to the historical partner reference chain is not independently
  established here; output ownership wording does not prove unrestricted CC BY eligibility.
  [BFL API service terms](https://bfl.ai/legal/flux-api-service-terms) are additional,
  not a replacement for the developer agreement.
- [Google API additional terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms), “Use of
  Generated Content”: Google does not claim ownership, similar outputs may occur,
  and applicable-law/returned attribution obligations still apply. This summary
  does not independently establish every partner-route or returned-attribution detail.
- [OpenAI Services Agreement](https://openai.com/policies/services-agreement/),
  effective January 1, 2026: section 4.1 assigns OpenAI's output rights, if any,
  to the customer to the extent permitted by law. Sections 4.3–4.4 retain input/use
  responsibility and warn outputs may not be unique. Section 3.3 restricts certain
  competitive-model uses; it is not an unconditional promise of all rights.
  [Service terms](https://openai.com/policies/service-terms/), updated October 16,
  2026, add conditions and exclusions; modified/combined outputs do not receive a
  blanket non-infringement guarantee. These official pages were read in a browser
  without sign-in. This summary does not independently establish the actual
  Comfy partner/reference-chain application or declare generated artwork eligible.
- [CC BY 4.0 legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.en):
  grants only rights the licensor has, is irrevocable, requires attribution and
  change indication, and does not grant patents/trademarks or endorsement.

## Retained boundaries, not a further investigation hold

This preparation scope does not require further generated-art redistribution
eligibility investigation. That scope decision does not establish copyright,
waive provider obligations or supply exact-content publication consent.

1. Preserve the recorded source chain, applicable-term limitations and qualified
   license scope. Do not silently extend MIT or CC BY to rights not established here.
2. Inspect current artwork and every proposed documentation frame for third-party
   material, personal information, provenance requirements and metadata. Strip
   personal capture metadata; do not erase mandatory provenance or authenticity
   markings. If both cannot be satisfied, withhold the file.
3. Record approved file hashes, license scope, attribution, modifications and source
   references in the final inventory. Keep raw generation records private.
4. Complete canonical source/privacy review and obtain separate exact-content
   publication consent before a public write.
